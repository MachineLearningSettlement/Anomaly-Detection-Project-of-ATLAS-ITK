Developement of a multimodal data-generation and post-training pipeline to improve semantic retrieval of semiconductor copper pixel-sheet images for anomaly-

inspection systems such as PatchCore and AnomalyDINO. First, I used Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large in an image-to-image generation framework to expand the anomaly 

dataset: real normal pixel-sheet images provided the geometry, shape, and structural diversity, while real anomalous images of the corresponding class—Chemical 

Contamination, Dust, or Scratch—provided realistic anomaly morphology through IP-Adapter conditioning; the generation process preserved the copper pixel-sheet

structure and black background while producing 100 synthetic anomalous images per class (300 total). The resulting synthetic anomalies were combined with real

anomalous data, while synthetic and real normal images formed the overall normal-image pool. Second, I designed a DPO post-training pipeline for Llama 3.2 

Vision 11B to learn semantic image retrieval: preference data associate textual descriptions of pixel-sheet geometry, or geometry combined with anomaly type, 

with a matching image and multiple non-matching images. Each chosen/rejected combination is converted into a pairwise DPO sample, with the two candidate images 

presented simultaneously to the vision-language model and the desired preference represented by the textual response “Image 1” or “Image 2”; candidate ordering 

is randomized to prevent positional bias. The pretrained Llama 3.2 Vision backbone is frozen and LoRA adapters are applied to attention and feed-forward 

projection modules, while the DPO objective optimizes the policy to assign higher relative probability to the image matching the semantic description than to 

the rejected alternative. The resulting adapted model is intended to retrieve representative and geometrically diverse pixel-sheet images, enabling more 

systematic construction of balanced training and evaluation datasets for downstream anomaly-detection models.
